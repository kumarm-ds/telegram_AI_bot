1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-HP4520 is a hydraulic power pack designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Max Operating Pressure: 320 bar
  - Rated Flow Rate: 185 L/min
  - Motor Power: 45 kW, 3-phase, 415V, 50Hz
  - Reservoir Capacity: 600 L
  - Weight: 1,180 kg
  - Dimensions (L x W x H): 1850 x 1100 x 1400 mm
  - Ambient Operating Range: -5°C to 55°C
  - Filtration Rating: 10 micron, Beta 200
  - Noise Level: 78 dB(A) at 1m

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-HP4520 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
