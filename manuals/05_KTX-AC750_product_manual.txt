1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-AC750 is a rotary screw air compressor designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Free Air Delivery: 128 CFM @ 7.5 bar
  - Motor Power: 75 kW (100 HP)
  - Max Working Pressure: 10 bar
  - Tank Capacity: 500 L (integrated)
  - Cooling: Air-cooled
  - Noise Level: 72 dB(A)
  - Weight: 1,850 kg
  - Control: Kaltrix AirSense IoT Controller

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-AC750 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
