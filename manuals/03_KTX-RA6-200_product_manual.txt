1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-RA6-200 is a six-axis robotic arm designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Payload Capacity: 200 kg
  - Reach: 2,650 mm
  - Repeatability: ±0.06 mm
  - Axes: 6
  - Controller: Kaltrix RoboLogic RC-500
  - Power Requirement: 400V, 3-phase, 15 kVA
  - Mounting: Floor, ceiling, or wall
  - Protection Rating: IP65
  - Weight (arm only): 980 kg

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-RA6-200 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
