1. STARTUP SEQUENCE

  1. Confirm all guards and interlocks are in place.
  2. Switch on main power disconnect.
  3. Wait for the HMI/controller to complete self-test.
  4. Confirm no active fault codes are displayed.
  5. Begin normal operation per the process procedure for your site.

2. NORMAL SHUTDOWN SEQUENCE

  1. Complete or safely interrupt the current cycle.
  2. Bring the unit to idle/zero-motion state.
  3. Use the controller's stop function (not the emergency stop) for routine shutdown.
  4. Switch off main power if the unit will be idle for an extended period.

3. EMERGENCY STOP

  Press any red E-stop button to immediately halt all motion. Refer to the Safety Bulletin for this model before resetting.
