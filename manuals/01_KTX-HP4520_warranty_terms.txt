1. WARRANTY PERIOD

2. COVERAGE

  This warranty covers defects in material and workmanship under normal industrial use. 3. CLAIM PROCEDURE

  1. Contact Kaltrix Technical Support with the unit serial number and a description of the fault.
  2. A support engineer will attempt remote diagnosis using the Troubleshooting Guide.
  3. If a site visit is required, a certified engineer will be dispatched within the region's standard response window.
  4. Approved warranty repairs are performed at no charge for parts and labor within the coverage period.

4. EXTENDED WARRANTY

  An extended warranty of up to 12 additional months is available for purchase within 60 days of the original warranty expiry. Contact your Kaltrix account manager for pricing.
