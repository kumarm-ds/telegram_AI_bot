Q1: What is the standard warranty period for the KTX-PK900?
A1: The standard warranty is 18 months from the date of commissioning, provided the maintenance schedule has been followed. See the Warranty Terms document for full details.

Q2: Where can I find the error code list for the KTX-PK900?
A2: The full list of diagnostic codes is in the Troubleshooting Guide for this model, using the PK prefix.

Q3: How do I order spare parts?
A3: Spare parts can be ordered through the Kaltrix Customer Portal or by contacting techsupport@kaltrix-industrial.example with the part number and unit serial number.

Q4: Can I have a third party service my unit?
A4: Non-Kaltrix-certified service may void your warranty. Refer to the Warranty Terms document before authorizing third-party work.

Q5: How often should preventive maintenance be performed?
A5: Refer to the Preventive Maintenance Schedule document for this model — intervals range from daily checks to annual calibration.

Q6: Who do I contact for urgent breakdowns?
A6: Call the Kaltrix Technical Support line at +91-44-6821-0099 and have your unit serial number and the on-screen error code ready.
