Bulletin: TSB-VFD-573
Affected Serial Range: KTX-VFD450-2554 to KTX-VFD450-7211

1. ISSUE DESCRIPTION

  Units within the affected serial range may intermittently display error VFD-F031 (Ground fault detected on motor cable.) under sustained heavy-duty operation. This is caused by a batch variance in a supplier component identified during routine quality review.

2. RECOMMENDED ACTION

  Kaltrix-certified engineers should inspect affected units at the next scheduled maintenance visit. No immediate shutdown is required unless the fault is actively occurring. Replacement parts are available at no charge for units still within the warranty period; reference the Spare Parts Catalog for the relevant part number.

3. CUSTOMER ACTION REQUIRED

  Check your unit's serial number against the affected range above. If your unit falls within this range, contact Kaltrix Technical Support at techsupport@kaltrix-industrial.example to schedule an inspection.
