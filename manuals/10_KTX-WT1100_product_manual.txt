1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-WT1100 is a industrial water filtration & treatment unit designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Treatment Capacity: 1,100 L/hour
  - Filtration Stages: 5 (sediment, carbon, RO, UV, remineralization)
  - RO Membrane Rejection Rate: 98.5%
  - Power Requirement: 230V single phase, 3.2 kW
  - Feed Water Pressure Requirement: 2.5 - 6 bar
  - Recovery Rate: 65%
  - Control: Kaltrix AquaLogic touchscreen controller
  - Footprint: 1.2m x 0.8m

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-WT1100 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
