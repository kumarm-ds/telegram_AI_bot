Preventive Maintenance Schedule
Product Line: CNC Machine Tools
Model: KTX-CNC880
Document ref: KTX-DOC-KTX-CNC880-PRE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MAINTENANCE INTERVALS

  - Daily: Visual inspection for leaks, unusual noise, or vibration.
  - Weekly: Check and log key operating parameters against baseline.
  - Monthly: Inspect and clean filters/sensors listed in the Spare Parts Catalog.
  - Every 500 operating hours: Replace consumable items (see Spare Parts Catalog, Section 1).
  - Every 2,000 operating hours: Full inspection by a certified Kaltrix service engineer.
  - Annually: Calibration check of all sensors and safety interlocks.

2. RECOMMENDED CONSUMABLES PER SERVICE CYCLE

  - KTX-CNC-ENC-Z: Z-axis absolute encoder assembly
  - KTX-CNC-CLP1: Coolant pump, 1.5kW
  - KTX-CNC-ATC-ARM: Automatic tool changer arm assembly

3. NOTE

Failure to follow this maintenance schedule for the KTX-CNC880 may affect eligibility for warranty claims. Maintenance records should be retained and made available to Kaltrix service engineers upon request.
