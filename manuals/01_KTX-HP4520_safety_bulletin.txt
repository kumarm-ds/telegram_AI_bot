Classification: Mandatory Read — All Operators

1. SAFETY SUMMARY

All personnel operating the KTX-HP4520 must complete Kaltrix operator safety induction before independent use. Required personal protective equipment (PPE) includes safety glasses, hearing protection where noise levels exceed 80 dB(A), and steel-toe footwear at all times near the unit.

2. LOCKOUT-TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE

  1. Notify all affected personnel before starting LOTO.
  2. Shut down the unit using the normal stop procedure.
  3. Isolate the main electrical disconnect and apply a personal lock and tag.
  4. Verify zero energy state before beginning any maintenance work.
  5. Remove lock/tag only after work is complete and the area is confirmed clear.

3. KNOWN HAZARDS SPECIFIC TO THIS MODEL

  Stored hydraulic pressure can remain in the system after shutdown — always relieve pressure via the manual bleed valve before disconnecting any line.
