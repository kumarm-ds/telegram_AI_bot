Kaltrix Industrial Systems
==========================
Installation & Commissioning Guide
Product Line: Hydraulic Systems
Model: KTX-HP4520
Document ref: KTX-DOC-KTX-HP4520-INS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SITE REQUIREMENTS

  - Confirm floor loading capacity meets or exceeds the unit weight listed in the Product Manual.
  - Ambient conditions must be within the operating range specified for the KTX-HP4520.
  - Electrical supply must match: 45 kW, 3-phase, 415V, 50Hz.

2. COMMISSIONING SEQUENCE

  Step 1: Unpack and inspect the unit for shipping damage. Report any damage to Kaltrix Logistics within 48 hours.
  Step 2: Position the unit per the foundation/anchor drawing supplied with the shipment.
  Step 3: Connect main power supply and verify phase rotation before first start.
  Step 4: Perform a no-load functional test using the Operator Quick Reference Card sequence.
  Step 5: Record commissioning data (serial number, firmware version, initial readings) in the Kaltrix commissioning log.
  Step 6: Contact Kaltrix Technical Support to schedule the mandatory 30-day post-commissioning inspection.

3. COMMON COMMISSIONING ISSUES

  - If error HP-101 appears during first start: Reservoir oil level below minimum threshold (sensor LS-1).
  - If error HP-114 appears during first start: Oil temperature exceeded 68°C — thermal cutoff triggered.
