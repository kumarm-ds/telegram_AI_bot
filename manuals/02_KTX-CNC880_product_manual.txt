1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-CNC880 is a cnc vertical machining center designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Table Size: 1000 x 500 mm
  - X/Y/Z Travel: 800 / 500 / 550 mm
  - Spindle Speed: 12,000 RPM max
  - Spindle Motor: 15 kW, direct drive
  - Positioning Accuracy: ±0.005 mm
  - Tool Magazine Capacity: 24 stations
  - Rapid Traverse Rate: 36 m/min (X/Y), 24 m/min (Z)
  - Controller: Kaltrix NextGen CNC-9 (Fanuc-compatible G-code)
  - Weight: 6,400 kg

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-CNC880 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
