1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-VFD450 is a variable frequency motor drive designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Power Rating: 450 kW
  - Input Voltage: 380-480V, 3-phase
  - Output Frequency Range: 0-590 Hz
  - Overload Capacity: 150% for 60 sec
  - Cooling: Forced air with internal fan
  - Protection Rating: IP21 (panel), IP54 optional
  - Communication: Modbus RTU, Profibus, Ethernet/IP
  - Weight: 185 kg

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-VFD450 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
