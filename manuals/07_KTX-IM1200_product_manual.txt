1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-IM1200 is a plastic injection molding machine designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Clamping Force: 1,200 kN
  - Screw Diameter: 50 mm
  - Max Injection Volume: 285 cm³
  - Injection Pressure: 180 MPa
  - Platen Size: 710 x 710 mm
  - Max Mold Height: 500 mm
  - Drive System: Servo-hydraulic hybrid
  - Control: Kaltrix MoldLogic HMI

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-IM1200 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
