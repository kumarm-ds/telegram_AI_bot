1. OVERVIEW

The Kaltrix KTX-CV3000 is a heavy-duty belt conveyor system designed for continuous-duty industrial operation in engineering and manufacturing facilities. It is engineered and assembled at the Kaltrix facility in Oragadam, Tamil Nadu, and is certified for use in demanding production environments.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  - Belt Width: 900 mm
  - Max Load Capacity: 3,000 kg/m run
  - Belt Speed Range: 0.2 - 2.5 m/s (VFD controlled)
  - Drive Motor: 7.5 kW, 415V
  - Frame Material: Hot-dip galvanized steel
  - Standard Section Length: 3 m (modular, up to 60m runs)
  - Incline Capability: Up to 18°
  - Control: Kaltrix ConveyLogic PLC panel

3. STANDARD ACCESSORIES INCLUDED

  - Operator quick reference card
  - Startup toolkit
  - 5-metre power supply cable
  - Digital copy of full documentation set (USB)

4. INTENDED USE

The KTX-CV3000 is intended for use by trained personnel in industrial settings only. It must not be operated outside the parameters listed in Section 2 above. Any modification voids the Kaltrix warranty described in the Warranty Terms document for this model.
